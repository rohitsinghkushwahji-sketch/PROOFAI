Seven checks, kept separate on purpose

A claim that nobody can confirm is not the same as a claim that is wrong. ProofAI shows the difference every time.

Supported

Evidence-supported claims

Claims backed by named, checkable evidence, shown with what supports them.

Contradicted

Contradicted claims

Claims that reliable evidence directly disproves, with the correction.

Unverified

Unverified claims

Not enough evidence either way. Flagged as uncertain, never as false.

Misleading

Misleading or missing context

Technically true statements that leave out what a reader needs to know.

Opinion

Opinions and assumptions

Judgements and hidden premises, kept out of the fact count.

Outdated

Outdated information

Claims that were once right but have since changed, with the date of change.

Sources

Source reliability

How trustworthy each cited source is, and why that rating was given.
